## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- **A bell when the hold reaches its target** in Wim Hof practices, to hold with eyes closed. It rings even with breath sounds off. (#2)
- **The breath ball breathes as in SwissZen:** a solid lit sphere that fills the ring on full lungs and shrinks to about a third between breaths, smaller still on empty lungs. The pet breathes inside it, growing and shrinking with it.
- **«Ring now»** in the bell settings, to hear the bell and see its notification at once.
- **A backup in one file:** Settings → More → Backup saves the practices, the diary, own practices and affirmations, the bottles, the pets, the tea house and the settings to a file, and loads them back on another phone. Before loading, the app says what is in the file; loading replaces what is on the phone.
- **Lingvale can move Puffling to another phone:** the village may ask for the same backup file and bring it back, together with the other apps of the family. It asks you before it loads anything.

### Fixed
- **The mindfulness bell was never heard.** It sounded on the media volume, which many keep at zero, and its notification is silent. Now it rings on the notification volume, and buzzes where that volume is down. After an update of the app or a change of the clock the next bell is set again, and the bell is no longer cut short. (#4)
- **The rock garden hung in the air,** and the bonsai and the candles with it: they now stand on the window sill. (#3)

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

The first release of Puffling.

- Paced breathing with the leading pet as the pacer: eight techniques (box, 4-7-8, coherent, physiological sigh, long exhale, triangle, alternate nostril, humming), breath sounds, haptics, and a «phone on your belly» mode that counts real breaths a minute.
- Wim Hof with the SwissZen engine: four levels with hold targets and your own tempo; the hold is a dive, and 16 sea finds fill an aquarium.
- Silent sitting with interval bells, guided practices (body scan, walking, grounding, a cup of tea, candle, noting, listening, the sky, gratitude, loving-kindness) and the «Rough sea» button for hard moments.
- A mindfulness bell at random times of the day that opens three breaths; quiet during calls and in silent mode.
- 120 affirmations in eight themes plus your own, said aloud and heard by the phone; every phrase sails off in a bottle and washes back weeks later.
- A diary of feelings: six families with exact words, strength, causes and text; a mood map in the summary.
- Own practices of every kind; six courses; a breath-hold test.
- 23 pets with abilities and natures, eggs found by how you practise, growth at night; a cliff tea house with the real sky and weather, a weekly tide and 16 things to buy with shells.
- A summary of minutes, what helps (tension before and after), holds, breaths a minute and times of day; seven kinds of share cards; first-visit hints on every screen.
