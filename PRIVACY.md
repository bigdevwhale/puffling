# Privacy

Puffling has no account and no analytics. Your practices, diary entries, affirmations, pets and settings stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

Only one thing goes over the network: **the weather for the round window.** The coordinates of the town you chose (or the one guessed from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached. Nothing else goes with them.

When you say an affirmation aloud, the phone's own speech recogniser hears it (on many phones it works offline; otherwise it is the recogniser's provider, not Puffling, that processes the audio). Puffling gets back only the recognised text, compares it with the phrase and keeps nothing else. The app doesn't ask for the microphone permission.

The «phone on your belly» mode reads the accelerometer only while a practice is playing. Notifications are used for the mindfulness bell and for a practice playing with the screen off. Share cards leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
